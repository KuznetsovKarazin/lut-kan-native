IEEE SENSORS JOURNAL — AUTHOR REQUIREMENTS CHECK
Checked 9 November 2026

Official guide:
https://ieee-sensors.org/ieee-sensors-journal/for-authors/
The guide requires the IEEE double-column template and a graphical abstract.
It normally limits an initial regular manuscript to eight pages; overlength
charges apply above eight published pages. It does not explicitly require an
author biography or photograph for the initial submission.

Official May 2024 JSEN template, independently verified archive:
IEEE-JSEN-LaTeX-template-202405.zip
SHA-256: 4ae0c0672b36a8463e1e159e181b69087c8a25dd71aefe75e0d0058936527755
The jsen.tex example says authors may include biographies (around line 770),
and describes the photograph conditionally (around line 792). These are examples
and formatting instructions, not an unconditional initial-submission mandate.

IEEE Editorial Style Manual for Authors, updated 29 July 2024, printed p.16:
https://journals.ieeeauthorcenter.ieee.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/7/IEEE-Editorial-Style-Manual-for-Authors.pdf
The manual explicitly allows all authors to omit biography and photograph,
without an unavailable-biography placeholder in that case.

Decision for this working version
The absence of a biography/photo is not established as a submission defect by
these sources. Keep the current manuscript unchanged on this point; do not
invent a portrait or degree chronology. Recheck any required fields in the
actual submission portal at submission. If later included, a professional
photo should be at least 300 dpi and fit 1 x 1.25 inches per the JSEN template.
This check does not claim journal acceptance or completion of submission.
